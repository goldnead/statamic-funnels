# Statamic Funnels — Marketplace

## Price

**$149.** Decided by Adrian on 2026-08-25.

The most expensive thing in this family, and the reasoning is not effort — it is what it replaces.
A funnel builder with pages, forms, offers and payments is what people rent Kajabi for, at somewhere
around €150 a month. This is bought once, runs on a site the owner already has, and the visitor data
stays there.

Against the siblings: consent $39, booking and payments $49 each, offers $29. Those each do one
thing. This one is the reason to have the others.

## What it needs

`goldnead/statamic-payments`, `goldnead/statamic-offers`, `goldnead/statamic-flow-canvas` — all
public on Packagist, all installed by Composer along with it. A buyer types one command.

## What it is not

A shop. There is no cart, no stock, no tax table, no shipping. A funnel sells one thing at a time,
which is what a funnel is; a site that needs a cart needs a different addon.
